What a move in clean covers in a Wellington house

The point of cleaning an empty house is reach. Cabinets and drawers get done inside, not just fronted. Appliance interiors, closet shelves, bathrooms, floors and every surface that a sofa would otherwise be standing in front of. That part is the same anywhere. What we treat as a headline here, rather than an extra, is the working end of the house: the mudroom, the laundry, the garage and the back door everybody actually uses.

Those rooms take the brunt in a horse town. The washer and dryer have been handling saddle pads and wraps, so the drums, the seals and the lint trap need attention rather than a glance. Boot trays, utility sinks and garage shelving hold whatever came off the last tenant's kit. Tell us if the property is let furnished, since that changes the job, and say if you want the move out at your old address quoted at the same time.

  • Kitchen cabinets, drawers and pantry shelving, inside
  • Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors
  • Mudroom, boot area and utility sink
  • Laundry room, including inside the machines and the lint trap
  • Garage shelving and the floor by the back door
  • Bathrooms, closets, skirting and screened lanai

The season sets the dates, and the dates do not move

Wellington describes itself as a year-round equestrian community, with more than 57 miles of trails threaded through the Equestrian Preserve, but the population still swells for the winter circuit. Tenants arrive in December and January, leave in the spring, and landlords are left with a very short gap between one set of keys and the next. Nobody in that chain has slack in the schedule, least of all the person unpacking.

So book early for January. Really early. We take bookings 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and we work evenings and weekends, which is what a two-day turnaround actually requires. Give us the handover date, the movers date and the time the new tenant lands, and we will build the clean into the only window that exists rather than the one that would be convenient.

Barn dust is not house dust

Arena footing, shavings, hay chaff and dried mud all travel indoors on boots, jackets, dog coats and car mats. It behaves differently from household dust: it is coarser, it collects in the grout by the back door, and it settles in the laundry room faster than anywhere else in the house. If you have just taken on a place that was let to a riding family, expect the working rooms to need more of the visit than the living room does.

It is also not inert. UF/IFAS veterinarians point out that mould and organic dust in hay and straw are the common triggers of equine asthma, which is a useful reminder that this material does something once it is airborne. We clean the house side of that line: floors, laundry, mudroom, surfaces, ventilation grilles. Barn aisles, stalls and wash racks are somebody else's specialty, and we will say so rather than pretend otherwise.

Farm properties, guest apartments and the office by the barn

A Wellington address often comes with more than one building. We are happy to quote the main house, a guest cottage, a groom's apartment, a barn office, a lounge or a barn bathroom, because those are living and working rooms with kitchens, showers and floors like any other. What we do not take on is the barn itself. Stalls, aisles, wash stalls and trailers are agricultural work with agricultural equipment behind it.

The rest is logistics, and on a farm the logistics are half the job. Which gate we use, where to park so a feed delivery is not blocked, which dogs are loose, whether the guest apartment shares a meter and has its own water on. Send it in writing when you book and it goes into the file for the property. Then a new person on the team learns the rules from a note rather than from getting one of them wrong.

Move out: leaving a furnished rental the way the inventory says you found it

Most season rentals here are let furnished, which makes the move out clean an inventory exercise as much as a cleaning one. Linens, upholstery, mattress protectors, outdoor furniture and the contents of a kitchen that came with the house all get looked at. Work through the inventory before we come, put anything broken on a list, and let the landlord hear it from you first. Surprises are what turn a handover into a dispute.

On deposits, we will not promise you anything, and nobody honest will. That decision belongs to the landlord and the lease. What a proper clean does is remove the obvious deductions: the oven, the refrigerator, the bathrooms, the laundry room, the floors. Photograph the property when we finish. If linens, upholstery or carpet need doing as well, ask when you get the quote and we will price the whole thing together.

Summer, storms and a house that nobody is in

From May onward, a lot of Wellington goes quiet. Houses sit closed with the air conditioning ticking over, which is exactly what should happen, because a sealed house at a sensible temperature stays under the humidity that mildew wants. Then hurricane season arrives, and on a horse property the house is usually last on a very long list. UF/IFAS publishes best practice guidance for horses in natural disasters.

We work around all of it. Book an opening clean a few days before you fly back rather than the morning of, so the house has time to settle and you are not meeting a cleaning crew with luggage in your hands. Pricing is flat, quoted upfront from real square footage, with an instant calculator on our homepage for home cleaning. Cleaners are background-checked, insured and trained, and if anything is not right, tell us within 24 hours and the re-clean is free.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a move in cleaning service in Wellington cost? It comes from square footage, the number of bathrooms and what you want included, quoted flat before we start. Our homepage calculator will give you a figure for home cleaning in about a minute. Extra buildings, a guest apartment or a garage that has been used for storage should be mentioned so they are priced in rather than added later.
Can you clean between two seasonal tenants over one weekend? Yes, if the dates are booked in advance. That weekend gap is the busiest slot we run in Wellington, and it fills up well before the season starts. Give us the checkout date and the arrival date, and we will schedule the clean into the gap, including a Saturday or a Sunday if that is what exists.
Do you clean barns, stalls or tack rooms? We clean living and working rooms: houses, guest apartments, barn offices, lounges and bathrooms. Stalls, aisles and wash racks are agricultural work and we do not take them on. A tack room can be quoted if it is a finished room with a normal floor. Describe what you have and we will tell you honestly where the line falls.
The rental is fully furnished. Does that change the clean? It changes the time and therefore the price. Furniture has to be worked around, and there is a lot more to wipe, lift and put back exactly where it was. Say so when you request the quote and it will be scoped properly from the start.
Can you wash the linens and towels between tenants? Yes, if it is agreed in the quote and the house has a working washer and dryer with time available on the day. Laundry takes hours that a cleaning schedule has to allow for, so it is not something we can add on the morning. Tell us how many beds and how many sets, and we will build the visit around it.
We are moving in with three dogs. Anything we should tell you? Just the practical parts. Whether the previous tenants had animals, whether there is carpet anywhere, and whether anybody in the household has allergies. Those answers change what we recommend for the bedrooms and the soft flooring. Carpet and upholstery work is quoted separately, so ask for it at the same time and we will price the lot.
How far ahead should I book for the winter season? Sooner than feels necessary. December and January dates in Wellington go early, and the good slots are the ones sitting in the gap between a checkout and a check-in. We answer the phone at any hour on (561) 556-5899, so there is no bad time to get a date on the calendar.
