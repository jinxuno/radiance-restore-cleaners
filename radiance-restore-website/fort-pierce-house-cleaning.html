House Cleaning in Fort Pierce, FL

Insured, background-checked cleaners who know what salt air, lagoon humidity and beach sand do to a Fort Pierce home.

Fort Pierce sits on the Indian River Lagoon in St. Lucie County, and the water shows up in the cleaning. Salt air drifts in off the Fort Pierce Inlet and settles on window glass, hinges and chrome. Sand comes home from South Beach and Jetty Park in shoes, towels and dog paws, then works its way to the floor edges and baseboards. Add humidity that hangs around most of the year, and a house here needs different attention than one further inland. That is the house we clean.

We are a small, family-run company working Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie and Fort Pierce, and Fort Pierce is its own job. The homes are older than most of the Treasure Coast. The median build year in the city is 1982, and close to half the housing stock went up before 1980, so we are usually working around original terrazzo and wood floors, painted trim, jalousie windows and grout that has stood in humid air for decades. We clean it carefully, quote flat pricing from your real square footage, and show up when we say we will.

Areas We Cover In and Around Fort Pierce

We clean across the city and the St. Lucie County areas around it. That includes historic downtown Fort Pierce around Second Street, Orange Avenue and the city marina, the Peacock Arts District, Lincoln Park north of Moore's Creek along Avenue D, and the older houses strung along Indian River Drive on the lagoon. We cross the causeways to South Hutchinson Island and North Hutchinson Island regularly, including South Beach, the condo buildings near Jetty Park and Fort Pierce Inlet State Park, and the rentals out past the Navy SEAL Museum. Inland we work White City, Indian River Estates, Lakewood Park and St. Lucie Village. If you are nearby and not listed here, call and ask.

Salt Air, Humidity and Sand: What They Do to a House Here

Salt does not rinse off on its own. In homes near the inlet and out on Hutchinson Island, it films the window glass, dulls chrome and faucets, and collects in sliding door and window tracks along with sand. Humidity keeps everything slightly damp, so surface mildew shows up first in grout lines, on shower ceilings, along caulk edges and around A/C vents and returns. We wipe those spots down and keep them dry instead of letting the film build. To be clear about what we are: we are cleaners, not a remediation or restoration contractor. If something looks past a wipe-down, we tell you so you can call a specialist.

Older Fort Pierce homes also need a lighter hand. Downtown and on the historic blocks we see original wood floors, terrazzo, painted trim and jalousie windows with a dozen slats each, finishes that will not survive harsh product or a scrub pad. We match the method to the surface, hand-wash blinds and slats rather than pushing dust around, and get into window sills, tracks and door casings where the sand and salt actually sit. Ceiling fans and vents get wiped, not just dusted, because year-round A/C keeps moving the same air through the house.

Every Service We Offer in Fort Pierce

  • Standard Cleaning in Fort Pierce

    Sand and salt film come back every week here. A recurring visit keeps floor edges, window sills and ledges, chrome and mirrors from hazing over between the bigger cleans. Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly schedules carry a discount.

  • Deep Cleaning in Fort Pierce

    This is the one that gets into the humid-climate problem spots: bathroom tile and grout, window and door tracks packed with sand, air vents and switch plates, and blinds and shutters hand-washed slat by slat instead of dusted.

  • Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning in Fort Pierce

    Fort Pierce runs close to half renters, so turnovers are constant. On a vacant home with power and water on, we take it down to bare surfaces, inside the refrigerator and every cabinet and drawer in kitchens that have absorbed years of coastal air.

  • Airbnb / VRBO Cleaning in Fort Pierce

    Beach rentals on Hutchinson Island turn over fast, and guests bring the sand in with them. We handle checkout to check-in the same day: floors mopped, glass and outdoor furniture wiped, bedding and bath mats laundered and replaced, beds made.

  • Post-Construction Cleaning in Fort Pierce

    Older houses here get renovated, re-roofed and storm-repaired often, and that dust settles into window and door tracks and vents where salt and sand already live. We inspect for dust and remove all of it, ideally before your furniture comes back.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning in Fort Pierce

Can you do anything about the mildew on my shower grout and caulk? We clean and disinfect tile walls, tubs, showers and shower doors, and on a deep clean we work the grout lines and caulk edges by hand. That takes care of surface mildew and slows it coming back. We are cleaners, not a remediation company, so anything behind the surface needs a specialist.
Do you clean out on Hutchinson Island and the barrier island condos? Yes. We cross the causeways to North and South Hutchinson Island, including South Beach and the buildings near Jetty Park. Island units need extra time on sliding door tracks, salt film on glass and railings, and sand along baseboards, so we plan the visit around that.
My house is older, with original floors and jalousie windows. Will you use the right products? Yes. Close to half of Fort Pierce homes were built before 1980, so we work on original terrazzo, wood and painted woodwork all the time. We match the product and method to the surface, hand-wash slats and sills, and keep anything abrasive away from older finishes.

Every cleaner who walks into your Fort Pierce home has been interviewed in person, background checked and reference checked, and works insured and trained. Pricing is flat and quoted up front from your real square footage, with no surprises at the end. If any part of the job is not right, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean it free.
