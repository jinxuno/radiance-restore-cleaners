What a deep clean covers that a standard clean does not

A standard visit is a maintenance job. It has a few hours and a house to get through, so it works on the surfaces that people touch and see. A deep clean has a different brief: go where the routine never has time to go, and take the build-up back to zero so the next twelve months of standard visits start from a decent baseline.

In practice that means the vertical and overhead work most people skip. Baseboards along the whole run, not just the visible stretch. Door frames and switch plates. Fan blades and light fixtures. Vent covers. The tops of kitchen cabinets. Shower grout and door tracks. Behind and beside the appliances, where a surprising archive accumulates. It takes longer, and it should.

  • Baseboards, door frames and trim throughout
  • Ceiling fan blades and light fixtures
  • Vent and register covers
  • Tops of kitchen cabinets and above the fridge
  • Shower and tub grout, door tracks and fixtures
  • Behind and beside movable appliances
  • Interior window sills and sliding door tracks

When to book a deep clean in Boca Raton

Deep cleaning Boca Raton homes follows the calendar, and two moments dominate it. October, when the house is coming out of a long humid summer and season is about to start, is far and away the busiest. The second is May, when people who go north want the place left properly before they close it up. Both are sensible. Both fill quickly, so a couple of weeks of notice helps.

Beyond that, the usual triggers apply: before family arrives for the holidays, after a remodel, after a long stretch with contractors in and out, or when you have simply had standard cleans for two years and want the reset. If you are already on a recurring plan, one deep clean a year is normally enough to keep the house from drifting.

One practical note about timing. Deep cleans take most of a day, sometimes more, so they do not slot into a schedule the way a routine visit does. Booking a fortnight ahead is comfortable most of the year and genuinely necessary in October. If you need one at short notice we will always look, but the honest answer in high season is often a week or two out.

Humidity, mildew and the 60 percent rule

Florida gives every bathroom a head start on mildew. The EPA recommends keeping indoor relative humidity below 60 percent, ideally between 30 and 50 percent, and a Boca Raton bathroom with a weak exhaust fan and a long shower is nowhere near that. The result is dark speckling along grout lines, in the caulk corner at the base of the tub, and on the ceiling above the shower head.

A deep clean addresses the surface build-up in those places, which is genuinely most of what people are looking at. What it is not is remediation. Florida's health department and the EPA both draw the line at roughly ten square feet: a small patch is a cleaning job, a larger or recurring one means you have a moisture source, and no amount of scrubbing outlasts a leak. We will tell you which one you have.

Deep cleaning a house that has been closed up for the summer

Plenty of Boca homes sit empty from May to October with the thermostat set high and the shutters closed. They do not come back the way they were left. There is a particular still, heavy smell that hits you in the entryway, a fine film on every horizontal surface, and five months of dust that has settled undisturbed and now, frankly, considers itself a resident with rights.

Accordion and louver shutter slats hold a summer's worth of grit on both sides. Lanai screens catch pollen, sand and whatever storms delivered. Ceiling fans that ran on low all summer wear a felt edge on every blade. Kitchen and bathroom drains that saw no water need running. We schedule these as a full-day deep clean rather than a normal visit, because trying to squeeze five months into three hours is how people end up disappointed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do deep cleaning services in Boca Raton cost? It scales with square footage and the condition of the house. We quote a flat price upfront rather than running an hourly clock, and the instant calculator on our homepage gives you a number without a sales call. A deep clean costs more than a standard visit because it takes considerably longer.
How long does a deep clean take? Longer than a standard visit, often two to three times as long for the same house. A home that has been closed up for the summer, or one that has never had a deep clean, takes the most. We will give you a realistic time window with the quote rather than an optimistic one.
How often should I deep clean my Boca Raton home? Once a year suits most households on a recurring plan. Twice a year makes sense for homes with pets, heavy tile, a lot of guests, or a long empty summer. If you are not on a recurring schedule at all, expect to want one more often than that.
Do I need to be home for a deep clean? No, though it helps to be reachable for the first one so we can ask about anything unusual. Many clients leave a key, a code or a lockbox. If your community has a guardhouse, put us on the approved visitor list beforehand or the gate will turn our cleaner away.
Will a deep clean get rid of mould in my bathroom? It removes surface build-up on grout, caulk and tile, which is what most people are actually seeing. It does not fix a moisture problem. The EPA and Florida Department of Health treat patches over about ten square feet, or anything that keeps returning, as a job for remediation. We will say so plainly.
Is deep cleaning the same as move-out cleaning? No. A deep clean is for a house you live in, so it works around furniture and belongings. A move-out clean is for an empty property and includes the inside of cabinets, closets and appliances because there is nothing in them. If you are handing over keys, book that instead.
Does a deep clean include carpets, tile restoration or exterior windows? No, those are quoted separately. Carpet extraction, tile and grout work, upholstery and exterior window cleaning are their own jobs with their own pricing and their own timing. Mention them when you book the deep clean and we will quote everything together, so you can make one decision instead of fielding three separate conversations.
