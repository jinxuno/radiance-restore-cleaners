Your Guide to Garage Cleaning in Palm Beach, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›Garage Cleaning

Garages swept, cleared of dust and cobwebs, shelving wiped, and floors cleaned. Often combined with organization service to make the space usable again.

A garage is one of the few spaces people book on its own. The house is fine, the garage is not, and it has been that way long enough to be irritating. Know what the service covers before you book it: this is cleaning and tidying, not hauling, not organizing, and not floor refinishing. We sweep and wash the slab, clear the cobwebs, wipe the shelving and workbenches, and put back whatever we moved. What we do not do is decide what stays and what goes, or carry off furniture, old paint, and the boxes you have been meaning to deal with since the last move. If a clear-out is what you actually need, that has to come first, and we work in behind it.

What's Included

  • Floors swept and washed by hand, including the edges and corners where grit builds up
  • Cobwebs cleared from ceiling corners, rafters, and along the garage door track and springs
  • Shelving, workbenches, and storage racks wiped down and put back in the order we found them
  • Garage door frame, interior door, and casings cleaned of dust, scuffs, and handprints
  • Water heater and laundry area surfaces dusted and wiped, including the top of the dryer
  • Oil spots and drips treated with degreaser and scrubbed, with honest limits on old staining
  • Swept debris bagged and removed; heavy junk hauling is not part of the service

What It Takes to Actually Get a Garage Slab Clean

A garage floor is bare concrete, which means it is porous and holds on to everything that lands on it. Sweeping alone burnishes fine grit into the surface. We vacuum the loose material first, treat oil and drip marks with degreaser and let it work, then scrub with a stiff brush and rinse in sections so dirty water never runs back over finished ground. Edges and the strip along the door threshold get done by hand, since that is where sand and leaf litter pack in.

Above the floor, the door track and springs carry a rope of dust and cobweb that nobody notices because it sits above eye level. That comes down before the slab is touched, along with the rafters and any exposed framing. Shelving is wiped one section at a time so your things go back where they were rather than in a pile. What a rushed visit does instead is a fast push-broom pass down the middle of the bay, which looks better for about a week.

Why This Matters in Palm Beach

In Palm Beach the garage is where hurricane season lives. Panels and shutters, plywood, gas cans, coolers, water cases, and a generator all get stacked in there from June onward, and the floor underneath disappears for months. The metal shutter panels are the worst of it. They come out of storage carrying grit and old salt, get leaned against a wall for the season, and leave rust lines and scrape marks on the slab and the drywall behind them. Come fall it all moves again, and what turns up is sand, leaf litter, and a season of grit ground across the concrete. A garage clean is easiest at either end of that cycle, before you stage supplies or after you put them away, and doing it beforehand means you are not sorting through a filthy corner in a hurry with a storm three days out. We sweep and wash the floor, clear the cobwebs off the door track and rafters, and wipe the shelving so you can see what you actually have before the next storm watch.

When to Book and What to Move First

Late spring is the obvious window. Getting the garage clean before the first watch of the season means supplies go onto clean shelving and you can find them in the dark. The other good window is late fall, once the panels and water cases are back in storage and the floor is clear again. Either way, book with a few days of flexibility, because a garage is far easier to clean well on a morning that has not already hit ninety degrees.

Before we arrive, move the cars out and unlock the interior door. If there is anything you would rather we did not touch, a workbench mid-project, a stack of paperwork, a project car under a cover, say so and we will work around it. Paint, solvents, propane, and pesticides stay exactly where they are and we clean around them. And if a corner has already been declared trash, put it at the curb first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you haul off the junk in my garage? No. We are a cleaning company, not a hauling service. We move items to sweep and wipe beneath them, then set them back where they were. For a full clear-out, bring in a junk removal company first and book us for the day after. An open floor cleans quickly.
Do you pressure wash or seal the garage floor? No. We sweep, scrub, and wash the slab by hand, using degreaser where it is needed. Pressure washing takes equipment and drainage we do not bring, and epoxy coating or sealing is contractor work with cure time behind it. Hand work gets concrete clean, but not new-looking.
What about paint, chemicals, or a pest problem? We do not move or dispose of paint, solvents, propane, or pesticides, and we do not treat insects or rodents. Those go to hazardous waste disposal and a licensed pest company. We will tell you plainly what we found and where, and come back once it has been handled.
Will the oil stains come out? Fresh drips usually do. Degreaser and scrubbing lift most of what has not had years to soak in. Older stains that have sat in porous concrete tend to lighten rather than disappear, and we will say so before we start rather than promise otherwise. We treat them regardless.
Can the garage be done during a regular house cleaning? It is booked separately. Standard and deep cleaning cover the interior living space, while a garage needs its own hours, different tools, and water at the slab. Plenty of customers schedule it alongside a deep clean on the same day. Mention it when you book so the time is allowed.

A garage that has gone a few seasons without attention is a bigger job than one done every spring, and the price reflects that, so describe it honestly when you call. The quote is flat, agreed before we start, and based on the real size of the space. Call (561) 556-5899 or book a garage cleaning online.
