What to Expect from Cleaning Services in Palm Beach, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›Cleaning Services

Residential cleaning built around your home and your calendar. Standard upkeep, deep cleans, move-in and move-out, and rental turnovers. Flat pricing quoted upfront, with payment collected after the work is finished.

People come to us at very different points. Someone has just closed on a house and wants it cleaned before the furniture lands. A family has an every other week routine and needs the heavy work handled twice a year. A homeowner is opening the place up in October after five months shut. A short term rental has a checkout at ten and a check in at three. Those are different jobs with different checklists, and booking the wrong one is the most common way people end up disappointed. This page lays out what each service actually covers, so you can pick the one that matches the state your house is in right now.

What's Included

  • Standard cleaning for kitchens, baths, floors, and surfaces on a repeating weekly to monthly schedule
  • Deep cleaning with hand-washed baseboards, blinds, vents, ceiling fans, and streak-free interior windows
  • Move-in and move-out cleaning for vacant homes, including inside cabinets, drawers, and the refrigerator
  • Rental turnovers with bedding and towels laundered and re-placed, dishes washed, garbage re-bagged
  • Post-construction cleaning with a full dust inspection and window and door track work
  • Kitchen detail on every service: appliance exteriors, stove top and drip pans, microwave inside and out
  • Bathroom detail on every service: tub, shower doors, tile walls, vanity, toilet, chrome shined
  • Flat pricing quoted upfront from real square footage, collected after the work is finished
  • Recurring discounts of 20% weekly, 15% bi-weekly, and 10% monthly on an ongoing schedule

How the Five Services Differ Once Someone Is in the House

Standard cleaning is maintenance. Floors, counters, sinks, tubs, toilets, mirrors, trash, beds made, appliance exteriors and the microwave inside. It assumes the house is already in reasonable shape and keeps it that way. Deep cleaning starts from that same list and adds the surfaces upkeep never reaches: baseboards hand-washed rather than wiped, blinds and shutters washed rather than dusted, ceiling fans, vents, switches and outlets, door casings including hinges and jambs, the inside of the oven, cabinet fronts, and interior windows done streak free.

Move-in and move-out is a deep clean of an empty house, plus the inside of the refrigerator and the inside of every cabinet and drawer, which is only possible once they are bare. It is performed on vacant homes with the power and water on. A turnover clean is built for speed and guest readiness, so laundry, dishes, and restocking sit alongside the surfaces. Post-construction begins with a dust inspection, then window and door tracks, blinds, and every horizontal surface.

Why This Matters in Palm Beach

Palm Beach runs on a season, and what a house needs depends entirely on where it sits in that cycle. Homes fill up through the fall and get closed again in late spring, and the two ends of that are opposite jobs. A closing-up clean is about leaving nothing behind that can turn while nobody is there: refrigerator emptied and wiped, trash out, garbage cans washed and relined, sinks and drains left clean, surfaces cleared. An opening clean is about five months of stillness. Shutters have been closed the whole time, so blinds, fans, sills, and interior windows carry a settled film, and the first thing an owner notices walking in is the light coming through dirty glass. Neither of those is the same as the every other week upkeep of a house lived in year round, where the work is floors, kitchen, and baths on repeat. Tell us which point in the season you are at and we scope the visit for that instead of guessing.

Choosing a Schedule That Matches the Season

For a house occupied year round, bi-weekly is the common choice and weekly suits households with kids, pets, or a pool. Recurring visits are discounted, 20% weekly, 15% bi-weekly, 10% monthly, and the flat price is still quoted upfront from your square footage. Most people start with one deep clean to reset the house, then move to standard visits, which costs less over a year than repeatedly booking deep cleans.

For a seasonal home, the pattern is different. Book a deep clean before the first night of the season, standard visits while you are here, and a final clean after you leave. If the house sits empty for months, a monthly visit through the off season keeps dust from settling into a layer. Tell us the arrival date when you book, since fall is the busiest stretch of our year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which service should I book first? Most homes start with a deep clean, particularly after being closed up for a few months. That handles baseboards, blinds, vents, ceiling fans, door casings, and appliance buildup once, so nothing is being caught up in pieces afterward. Standard cleaning on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly schedule then holds the house at that level.
How is the price worked out? Pricing is flat and quoted upfront from your home's real square footage rather than an hourly guess, and you pay after the work is finished. Recurring schedules take 20% off weekly, 15% off bi-weekly, and 10% off monthly. If the scope changes, the quote changes before the visit, never after it.
What happens if something gets missed? Tell us within 24 hours and we return and re-clean it at no charge. That is the entire guarantee and there is nothing else attached to it. It helps to walk the house the same day and point out anything that is not right, rather than mentioning it weeks later at the next visit.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning? No. Plenty of clients leave a code or a key arrangement and come back to a finished house, which is common for seasonal owners who are not in the state. If you are away, tell us where the alarm instructions are and which rooms matter most. We still ask that someone looks the house over within 24 hours.
Can one visit combine two services? To a point, yes. A standard visit can carry deep clean tasks added to it, so blinds get hand-washed one month and baseboards the next, and the quote reflects the added scope. What cannot be combined is move-in and move-out work with an occupied house, since cabinets and the refrigerator have to be empty.

Different houses need different work at different times of the year, which is the whole reason there are five services rather than one. Tell us the state your home is in, whether it is opening for the season, sitting empty, turning over between guests, or simply overdue, and we will point you at the right one. Book online or call to get that scoped.

For homes that need more than one task addressed, Deep Cleaning in Palm Beach is the service to book.
