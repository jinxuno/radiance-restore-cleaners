What to Expect from Holiday Cleaning in Fort Pierce, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›Holiday Cleaning

Get the house guest-ready before family arrives, or reset it after they leave. Kitchens, bathrooms, guest rooms, and entertaining spaces handled on your timeline, including tight-turnaround dates.

Holiday cleaning in Fort Pierce splits into two visits that do different jobs. The one before guests arrive is about rooms that normally sit unused: the spare bedroom, the second bathroom, the dining table nobody eats at in July. The one after is about a house that has been running at capacity for a week. Families with relatives flying south for two weeks tend to want the after visit. Anyone hosting a single big dinner usually wants the before. Owners who lend a place on Hutchinson Island to relatives often book either side of the stay. Tell us the arrival and departure dates and we will work backwards from them.

What's Included

  • Guest rooms cleaned, beds made, closet floors vacuumed, furniture and knick-knacks dusted
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected: tubs, showers, shower doors, tile walls, vanities and toilets
  • Kitchen ready to cook in: counters, backsplash, sinks, stove top, drip pans and the microwave
  • Floors swept, mopped and vacuumed, including stairs, under furniture and along the floor edges
  • Window sills, ledges, ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted and wiped before guests arrive
  • Trash emptied and garbage cans cleaned and relined, whether we come before or after
  • Slider tracks and entry thresholds cleared of the sand that comes back in on shoes
  • Dining and entertaining rooms dusted, mirrors and chrome shined, banisters wiped down

What a Guest-Ready Visit Covers, Room by Room

The guest room and guest bath come first, because they have been closed longest and they are where visitors form an opinion in the first ten minutes. Beds are made, closet floors are vacuumed, and the surfaces that collect a still-room film get dusted: nightstands, lamp bases, the blades of the ceiling fan, the sill behind the curtain. In the bath, the tub, tile walls, shower door glass and vanity are cleaned and disinfected, and the bin is emptied and relined.

Then the kitchen and the rooms people gather in. Counters and backsplash are degreased, the stove top and drip pans are lifted and cleaned, the microwave is done inside and out, and the top of the refrigerator gets wiped since nobody looks at it until they reach for a serving dish. Floors go last, edges included. The parts that quietly take longest are shower door glass, grout lines and slider tracks, and those are the three a quick service leaves alone.

Why This Matters in Fort Pierce

Holidays in Fort Pierce mean the door opens all day. People come back from South Beach and Jetty Park with sand in their shoes, and it ends up in the grout lines, along the floor edges and down in the slider tracks. Sand here is not dust. It is coarse, it scratches tile and wood underfoot, and it gets walked from the entry into every room within a day of arriving. Older houses near the historic downtown add jalousie windows and deep sills that catch it. Add a house full of people and a kitchen running from breakfast to late, and a home that was clean on Tuesday does not look it by Thursday. We focus on what guests actually use: the guest bath, the guest room, the kitchen, and the floors that take the traffic. That means vacuuming the edges and the tracks rather than just the open floor, since the open floor is the part that looks fine anyway. Book the visit before, the visit after, or both, and we will hold the dates.

Booking Around a Calendar That Fills Early

The last week of November and the stretch from mid-December into early January are the tightest of the year, and the two days either side of a holiday go first. Once you know when people are arriving, call with the dates even if the details are loose. Changing a booked time is far easier than finding one in the same week. If your dates are already taken, the day before usually still does the job.

You do not have to clean the whole house. Plenty of holiday bookings cover a guest room, a bathroom and the kitchen only, and the quote reflects the rooms we actually clean. If the hosting season is long enough that you want the house held steady through it, a recurring schedule is discounted 20 percent weekly, 15 percent bi-weekly and 10 percent monthly, and you can stop it when the season ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I book before guests arrive or after they leave? Plenty of people do both. The visit before sets up the guest rooms and bathrooms. The visit after resets the kitchen, the laundry and the floors. If you only want one, the after visit usually carries more weight, because a house that has held eight people for a week needs more than one that is expecting them.
How far ahead should I schedule a holiday date? As early as you can. Late November through early January books up, and the days right before and right after a holiday go first. Call once you know your dates and we will hold what is still open. If your first choice is taken, we will offer the nearest day that genuinely works for the crew.
Can you clean only the guest rooms and bathroom? Yes. Tell us which rooms to cover and we will quote just those. Pricing is flat and based on real square footage, so a partial-home clean is priced for what we actually clean. It is a common request in December, when the rest of the house is fine and only the spare rooms have sat closed.
Do you wash dishes and change the beds after guests leave? Dishes are washed and put away as part of the visit. Stripping guest beds and laundering the bedding, bath mats and towels is turnover work we do regularly, so ask for it when you book and we will build the time into the quote rather than fitting it in on the day.
Can you come while the house still has guests in it? Yes, though it works better if everyone is out for a few hours. We can clean around people, but rooms occupied with luggage on the floor get a lighter clean by necessity. If a mid-stay visit suits you, pick a morning when the household has plans and tell us which doors to leave shut.

The dates that matter to you matter to everyone else on the same street, so the calendar is the real constraint here, not the cleaning. Ring (561) 556-5899 or send your dates to admin@radiancerestore.org as soon as travel plans are settled, and we will tell you what is still available on either side of the visit. Getting on the schedule early is the whole trick.

This is one piece of a larger job. Deep Cleaning in Fort Pierce is the full service most clients book alongside it.
