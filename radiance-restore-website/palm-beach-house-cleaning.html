House Cleaning in Palm Beach, FL

Careful, insured cleaners for Palm Beach estates, historic Mediterranean homes and seasonal residences, from Worth Avenue to the North End.

Palm Beach is not a town of identical floor plans. A house a few blocks off Worth Avenue may have a 1920s stucco shell, a barrel tile roof, marble laid before air conditioning existed, and a pecky cypress ceiling nobody can reach without an extension pole. The next street over is a mid-century ranch with terrazzo running wall to wall. We look at the house before we quote it, because the difference between a small in-town cottage and a nine-thousand-square-foot estate is not only hours on the clock. It changes the products, the tools and the size of the crew.

We also work both halves of the Palm Beach calendar. From November through April the island fills up, houses are lived in hard, guests come and go, and the standard has to hold every week. Through the summer many of those same houses sit closed with the shutters fastened and the thermostat set high. Each state needs attention and each needs a different kind. Every cleaner we send has been interviewed in person, background checked and reference checked, and arrives insured and trained.

Neighborhoods and Areas We Serve on the Island and Nearby

On the island we clean the Estate Section between Worth Avenue and Sloan's Curve, the in-town blocks from Worth Avenue up to Wells Road, and the North End from Wells Road out to the inlet. That includes the streets off County Road and North Lake Way near the Lake Trail, the shops and apartments around Royal Poinciana Way and Sunset Avenue, the condominiums along South Ocean Boulevard in the South End, and the smaller waterfront pockets of Everglades Island and Ibis Isle. Across the bridges we work the 1920s houses of El Cid and Flamingo Park in West Palm Beach, and we take jobs in Wellington, Royal Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens and Boynton Beach. If your street is not listed, call and ask.

Estate, Landmark and Luxury Home Cleaning in Palm Beach

Older Palm Beach houses are full of surfaces that punish the wrong product. Mediterranean Revival homes from the 1910s through the 1930s left the town stucco walls, arched loggias, wrought iron rails, hand-painted stair tile, pecky cypress beams and marble that has never been sealed with anything modern. Acidic and vinegar-based cleaners etch marble. Too much water swells antique woodwork and sits in terrazzo seams. We use pH-neutral products, soft microfiber, dry dusting on old wood, and we damp wipe rather than soak. If a surface is unfamiliar, we ask before we touch it.

Scale is the other half of the job. Twelve and fourteen foot ceilings put fans, chandeliers, crown detail and the tops of tall casework out of one person's reach, so those rooms get two cleaners and proper poles instead of a ladder shuffle. Long loggias, guest wings and back stairs add real square footage that a walk-through catches and a phone estimate does not. Seasonal owners usually want two big cleans a year: one after they leave, before the house is shut for the summer, and one in the fall before the family and the guests come back.

Every Service We Offer in Palm Beach

  • Standard Cleaning in Palm Beach

    The workhorse from November through April, when the house is occupied and entertaining. Floors, sills, banisters, baths, kitchen and beds every visit. Weekly and bi-weekly schedules carry a standing discount and keep in-season upkeep predictable.

  • Deep Cleaning in Palm Beach

    Older island houses hide dirt in the detail: louvered shutters, deep baseboards, hinges and door casings, ceiling fans, air vents. Deep Cleaning hand-washes shutters and blinds instead of dusting them and scrubs baseboards by hand.

  • Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning in Palm Beach

    Palm Beach property changes hands and changes tenants constantly, and estates often sell furnished then empty. This is the vacant-home clean, cabinets and drawers and refrigerator interior included, done with power and water still on.

  • Airbnb / VRBO Cleaning in Palm Beach

    Seasonal rentals here run back to back through the winter months. We do same-day checkout-to-check-in turnovers, launder and replace bedding, mats and towels, wipe down outdoor furniture, and restock supplies on request.

  • Post-Construction Cleaning in Palm Beach

    Renovation is constant on the island, and landmark-era work throws plaster and masonry dust that settles on beams, in window and door tracks and behind blinds. Best booked once trades finish and before furniture and art return.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning in Palm Beach

Can you clean marble, terrazzo and old hardwood without damaging them? Yes. We use pH-neutral cleaners on stone, never acidic or vinegar-based products, and we damp mop rather than flood. Old hardwood and antique woodwork get dry dusting and light microfiber work. We clean these surfaces. We do not grind, refinish or seal stone.
We close the house for the summer. Can you clean before we leave and again before we return? That is a common booking here. Most seasonal owners take a Deep Cleaning after they head north so the house is shut clean, then another before they fly back in the fall. If the house is fully vacant with power and water on, Move-Out level covers cabinets and refrigerator interiors too.
How do you handle very large homes and high ceilings? We walk the house, measure the real square footage and quote a flat price up front, then size the crew to match. Rooms with twelve-foot-plus ceilings, tall fans and chandeliers get two cleaners and extension tools so nothing is skipped or rushed.

Every cleaner who carries a key to a Palm Beach house has been interviewed face to face, background checked and reference checked, and comes to you insured and trained. If any part of the work misses the mark, tell us within a day of the visit and we return and redo that area free. Pricing stays flat and is quoted from measured square footage before we start.
