Website Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Radiance Restore Cleaners is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

What We're Doing

We regularly review our site's structure, color contrast, and navigation to improve accessibility for screen readers, keyboard navigation, and other assistive technologies.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. If you encounter any barrier while using this site, please let us know so we can address it.

Contact Us

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, please contact us at admin@radiancerestore.org or call (561) 556-5899, and we'll work with you to provide the information or service you need.
