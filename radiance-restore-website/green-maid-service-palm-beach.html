How Green Maid Service Works in Palm Beach, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›Green Maid Service

Ongoing housekeeping using eco-conscious products. Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly service that keeps the home clean without introducing harsh chemicals into your living space.

Recurring service is a different decision from a one-off clean. You are choosing who walks through the house every week or two, what they bring with them, and what gets left behind in the air and the drains over a year. Most of the households booking this have made that call deliberately. Some have small children or animals. Some have been through a service that arrived with a bucket of something eye-watering and decided once was enough. What they want is the same full checklist a conventional maid service runs, on a fixed schedule, done with eco-conscious products and by somebody who learns the house instead of meeting it fresh every time.

What's Included

  • Recurring weekly, bi-weekly or monthly visits working the same full checklist every time
  • Eco-conscious products used throughout the house, with fragrance-free or low-VOC available on request
  • Floors vacuumed under furniture, swept and mopped on every single visit without exception
  • Beds made, wastebaskets emptied, garbage cans washed out and relined each time
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, chrome shined, mirrors and glass doors finished streak-free every visit
  • Appliance exteriors, stove top, drip pans and microwave cleaned inside and out
  • Furniture, woodwork, shelves, door frames and banisters dusted, sills and ledges included
  • Countertops, backsplash and sinks cleaned and disinfected, with stairs and upholstery vacuumed as well

What Forty or Fifty Visits a Year Looks Like

A recurring clean is not a smaller clean, it is a maintained one. The same list runs each visit, which means the surfaces that get skipped in most houses simply never fall behind. Baseboards do not build a rim of grit. Shower doors do not reach the point where they need a scraper. That is why a recurring visit takes less time than a deep clean without cutting anything: the crew is holding a standard rather than digging out of a hole. The first visit is usually the exception, and we will say so when we quote.

Product choice compounds at that frequency. Whatever gets used goes onto the same counters and down the same drains fifty times a year, so choosing a milder product is a meaningfully different decision from using one once. We carry eco-conscious products across the whole checklist, keep bathroom cloths and kitchen cloths in separate sets, launder every cloth and mop head between homes, and change mop water between rooms rather than pushing the same grey bucket around the house.

Why This Matters in Palm Beach

Recurring service is where product choice actually adds up. A house cleaned weekly gets forty or fifty visits a year, and whatever is used in it ends up in the air, the fabric and the drains over that stretch. Households out toward Wellington and Royal Palm Beach also deal with barn grit and arena sand riding in on boots that never quite come off at the door, so floors need genuine work every visit rather than a light pass. That sand is coarse and it travels. It collects along baseboards, in the tracks of sliding doors and in the seam where tile meets carpet, and if it is left it gets ground into finishes underfoot. Horse country households tend to keep a mud room or a laundry entry doing heavy duty, and those rooms earn a proper share of the visit rather than a token wipe. We use eco-conscious products across the whole checklist and keep the same cleaner on your home wherever scheduling allows it, so they learn which floors take what and where the grit hides.

Choosing Between Weekly, Bi-Weekly and Monthly

Weekly suits a full house: children at home, dogs in and out, or a property where grit gets tracked in every single day. It also takes 20% off the price. Bi-weekly is the most common choice by a distance and works for most families, at 15% off. Monthly, at 10% off, is for households already keeping up with the daily tidying who want the heavy surfaces properly reset on a schedule.

Start honestly rather than optimistically. If the house has not had a thorough clean in months, book a deep clean first and put the recurring schedule on top of it. A recurring visit is timed to maintain a house, not to catch one up, and stretching one to do both work leaves everything a little short. You can change frequency later. Plenty of clients run weekly through season and drop to monthly over summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I get the same cleaner each visit? We keep the same person on your home wherever scheduling allows, and a fixed weekly or bi-weekly slot usually makes that work. Every cleaner is background-checked, reference-checked, interviewed in person, insured and trained on the same checklist, so a cover visit still runs the same list in the same order.
What happens if something gets missed? Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean it free. That covers recurring visits exactly as it covers one-time cleans. Pricing is flat and quoted upfront from real square footage, so the return trip costs you nothing and does not shorten the next scheduled visit either.
Can I change the products partway through? Yes, at any point. Ask for fragrance-free or low-VOC across the whole visit, or only in certain rooms such as a nursery or a home office. You can also leave out your own products and we will use those instead, as long as you tell us what stays off which surface.
Do you bring your own equipment, and is it used in other homes? We bring our own products and equipment. Cloths and mop heads are laundered between homes rather than carried house to house dirty, and bathroom cloths never get used on a kitchen counter. If you would rather we run your vacuum or use products you keep in the house, leave them out.
Can I skip a visit when we are away? Yes, just give us reasonable notice and we will move or skip it. Some clients keep the visit while they are away, particularly before coming home from a long trip, since an empty house is quick to work and it is pleasant to walk back into. Either way the schedule picks up after.
