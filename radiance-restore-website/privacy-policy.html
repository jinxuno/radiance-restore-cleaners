Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Note: This page is a general template and has not been reviewed by an attorney. Before publishing this live, have a Florida-licensed attorney review it to make sure it actually fits how your business collects and handles customer information.

Radiance Restore Cleaners is operated by Radiance Restore LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, book a service, contact us, or apply for a position, we may collect: your name, phone number, email address, home or property address, and any details you provide about the service you're requesting (such as square footage, service type, or scheduling preferences).

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to: provide price estimates and quotes, schedule and perform cleaning services, communicate with you about your appointment, and, if you apply for a position, evaluate your application. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Information

Your information is shared with the service providers we use to run our business, specifically Jobber (our scheduling and quoting platform) and Zapier (which connects our website forms to Jobber), solely for the purpose of processing your request. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Florida Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals in accordance with the Florida Information Protection Act (FIPA), which generally requires notification without unreasonable delay and no later than 30 days after discovery of the breach, unless a longer period is authorized by law enforcement.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information can be directed to admin@radiancerestore.org or (561) 556-5899.
