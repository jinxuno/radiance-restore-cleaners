Move-in cleaning in Boca Raton: what an empty home gets

An empty home is the only chance you will get to clean everything at once, so we take it seriously. Cabinets and drawers are cleaned inside. Closet shelving gets done, including the high shelf that becomes unreachable the moment a bed goes in. Appliance interiors, bathroom tile, shower glass, toilets, baseboards, door frames, switch plates and floors. Blinds and window sills, which collect a decade of dust in the slats.

South Florida water is hard, so shower glass and faucets in a home that has been used for years usually carry mineral film rather than dirt. That responds to proper cleaning rather than pressure, and it is one of the differences you feel immediately when you move in. If the previous occupant left the oven in a state, tell us before the visit so we scope it properly rather than discover it at nine in the morning.

  • Inside all cabinets, drawers and pantry shelving
  • Oven, refrigerator and dishwasher interiors
  • Shower glass, tile, faucets and fixtures
  • Blinds, window sills and slider tracks
  • Closet shelves, rods and top shelves
  • Baseboards, door frames and switch plates

Move-out cleaning and the security deposit clock

Let us be honest about what a cleaning company can and cannot promise. We cannot guarantee your deposit comes back. That is your landlord's decision, and it depends on the lease, the condition of the unit and a hundred things outside a mop's control. What a proper move-out clean does is remove the easy, obvious deductions: the oven, the fridge, the bathrooms, the grease above the range, the marks along the baseboards.

Florida law does set the timetable. Under Florida Statutes section 83.49, a landlord who intends to keep any part of a deposit must give the tenant written notice within 30 days of the tenancy ending, and must return an unclaimed deposit within 15 days. Tenants then have 15 days to object in writing. Knowing those dates is worth as much as the clean, because it tells you when to start asking questions.

  • Book the clean after the last box leaves
  • Photograph the empty unit when we finish
  • Check what the lease specifically requires
  • Note the 15 and 30 day statutory deadlines
  • Ask us about carpet as a separate quote

Boca condo and HOA rules that change your moving schedule

This is the part that catches new arrivals out. Many Boca Raton condo associations restrict moves to weekday business hours, require the elevator to be reserved and padded in advance, and want the moving company's insurance on file before the truck reaches the loading area. Rental applications in association-governed communities can take weeks to approve, which quietly sets your real move date.

All of that affects when a clean can actually happen. Tell us the building's rules when you book and we will fit inside them, including morning slots before a move window opens or evening slots after it closes. We are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, which is less of a slogan than a practical answer to a building that only permits vendors between nine and four.

What the last tenant left behind

Empty rentals have a genre of their own. The refrigerator that was emptied but not wiped. The range hood filter nobody has ever removed. Slider tracks holding sand from a beach trip in 2019. A bathroom cabinet with a lone hair tie in it. None of this is unusual and none of it is a crisis. It is just a list of small jobs that add up to a home feeling used or feeling yours.

Then there is the smell question, which people ask more often than they admit. A closed-up unit smells of stale air and dry drain traps more than anything sinister, and cleaning plus ventilation usually sorts it. If there is genuine moisture damage behind it, cleaning is the wrong tool, and we will tell you rather than mask it. The Florida Department of Health publishes sensible homeowner guidance on when mould becomes a job for a specialist.

Products, pets and the first night in a new place

People care what gets used in a home they are about to sleep in, especially with small children or a nervous cat arriving the same week. If you have preferences, tell us when you book and we will talk it through. The EPA's Safer Choice programme is a good reference point if you want to understand what the labels on cleaning products actually mean rather than trusting the word natural on a bottle.

One practical note for move-in week: get the clean done before the furniture, if the timing allows it at all. It is faster, cheaper in effort, and infinitely better in result, because an empty room has no wall of boxes standing between a cleaner and the baseboard. Cleaning around your belongings is possible, and we will do it, but it is like painting a room with all the pictures still hanging on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a move-out clean get my security deposit back? We cannot promise that, and be wary of anyone who does. Your deposit is the landlord's decision under your lease and Florida Statutes section 83.49, which requires written notice of any claim within 30 days. A thorough clean removes the obvious deductions and gives you photographic evidence of the condition you left. That is what it can do.
Is move-out cleaning required by my lease in Boca Raton? It depends on your lease. Many Florida leases require the unit be returned in the condition it was received, allowing for normal wear, and some name professional cleaning specifically. Read the clause before you book so you know what standard you are working to, and tell us if it lists particular items.
Does a move-out clean include carpet cleaning? Carpet is quoted separately, because the work and the pricing are genuinely different. We do take carpet, tile, window and upholstery work, so you can arrange both with us at once. Mention the carpet when you request your move-out quote and we will price the whole job together.
Can you clean while there is still some furniture in the unit? Yes, though an empty home cleans better and faster. If a few pieces are staying, or the closing date leaves you with a sofa and no plan, just tell us what will be there when you book. We would rather quote accurately than adjust the scope with a crew standing in your living room.
My building only allows moves on weekdays. Can you work around that? Yes. Boca Raton condo buildings commonly limit moves and vendor access to weekday hours, with elevators reserved in advance. We book 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so we can clean early, late, or on the day either side of your move window. Tell us the building's rules when you schedule.
Do you clean garages, balconies and storage units? We can, but mention them when you ask for the quote so they are priced in. Garage floors and shelving take real time, especially if the previous occupant used the space for storage. Balconies near the coast usually need a wet clean rather than a sweep, thanks to salt film on railings and glass.
Do landlords and property managers book you directly? Yes, and it is often simpler that way for turnovers between tenants. We quote a flat price from real square footage, work to whatever schedule the unit's availability allows, and are open around the clock.
