What house cleaning services in Boynton Beach FL include

Most house cleaning in Boynton Beach FL comes down to two choices. A standard clean is the maintenance visit: kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, surfaces, beds made, bins out, the ordinary work that keeps a house level. A deep clean goes after what a weekly visit never has time for, like baseboards, door frames, inside the appliances, the tops of cabinets and the tile edges. Plenty of households begin with a deep clean, then settle onto a recurring schedule.

The rest are specific jobs. Move-in and move-out cleans for an empty house, turnover cleans for an Airbnb or seasonal rental, and post-construction cleans once the trades finally leave. We also take carpet, tile, window and upholstery work here, priced after somebody looks at it rather than guessed on the phone. If you are not sure which you need, describe the house and we will say which one it is, including when the cheaper option wins.

  • Standard recurring cleans, weekly through monthly
  • Deep cleans, once or twice a year
  • Move-in and move-out cleans
  • Airbnb and seasonal rental turnovers
  • Post-construction cleans after a remodel
  • Carpet, tile, window and upholstery work, quoted separately

East of Federal, west of Lyons: one city, two cleaning problems

Close to the water, the house fights the outdoors. Sand comes home from the inlet in shoes, towels and dog fur, and it goes straight into door tracks, tile grout and the mat inside the front door. Salt air leaves a haze on window glass and a chalky film on outdoor hardware. Homes here are often older and single-story, and the work is detailed rather than large.

Out west past Military Trail and Jog Road, the houses get bigger and the problem changes shape. Two stories, more bathrooms than people, a screened lanai facing a lake, and enough square footage that a thorough clean is a matter of hours and hands rather than fiddly detail. Pollen and lake breeze coat the lanai furniture, and the second-floor rooms nobody uses still collect dust. Same city, different quote, which is exactly why we price from real square footage.

Crumbs, ants and the wildlife that lets itself in

Let us settle something. A palmetto bug on your kitchen floor is not a verdict on your housekeeping. They fly in from the mulch, the storm drain and the garage, and they will happily walk into the cleanest house on the street. Ants are the same story with better organisation. What cleaning does is remove the reason they stay, which is food, grease and water in places nobody looks.

So we pay attention to the spots that matter: under and behind the toaster and the coffee machine, the gap beside the range, cabinet fronts near the handles, the pet bowl area, the bin and the floor beneath it, and the sink surround where water sits overnight. EPA pest control guidance says the same thing in fewer words: take away food, water and shelter. We are not a pest control company, but we work well alongside one.

  • Behind and under small kitchen appliances
  • The gap between the range and the cabinet
  • Pet bowl areas and the floor around bins
  • Sink surrounds, drain edges and the dishwasher seal
  • Pantry shelves and cabinet fronts
  • Laundry room floors and behind the machines

August in a Boynton house: humidity, the vents and the shower ceiling

Summer here is a long, damp test of every room without a window. Mildew shows up first in the same three places: the shower ceiling above the door, the grout at the base of the enclosure, and the wall behind furniture in a closed guest room. It arrives quietly and it arrives fast. The EPA suggests keeping indoor relative humidity between 30 and 50 percent, which is easier to say than to achieve in a Florida August.

Regular cleaning catches this early, which is most of the value. A cleaner wiping a shower ceiling every week sees the first speckle long before you do standing under the water. The CDC is blunt that mould grows wherever moisture sits, so the ventilation fan and a dry surface do more for you than any bottle. If something has gone past cleaning, into drywall or under a floor, we say so and point you to a remediation specialist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my quote different from my neighbour's? Square footage first, then the type of clean and the frequency. A 1,300 square foot villa near Congress Avenue and a 3,000 square foot two-story out past Lyons Road are not the same job even if both are immaculate. Number of bathrooms matters more than people expect, because bathrooms are the slowest rooms in any house.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment? Yes, we arrive with what we need. If you would rather we use your products, because of a stone floor, a finish you are protecting or an allergy in the house, leave them out and tell us. We would rather use your bottle than guess at a surface and be wrong about it.
What should I do before the cleaning team arrives? Very little. Clear the kitchen counters and the bathroom vanities if you can, since surfaces we can actually reach get cleaned better, and put away anything valuable or private. Tell us where the dog will be. Beyond that, nobody needs to tidy up for the cleaners, which rather defeats the purpose.
Is there anything you will not clean? Yes, and we would rather say so up front. We do not do mould remediation, pest control, roof or exterior pressure washing, or anything that needs a ladder taller than a step ladder. We also will not use a product on a surface it could damage. When a job needs a specialist, we say so instead of trying it.
Can a cleaning service help with an ant or palmetto bug problem? It helps, but it is not the fix. Cleaning removes the food and water that keep insects interested, particularly in kitchen gaps, pet areas and around bins. Actually treating an infestation is a pest control company's job. Many Boynton households run both, and the cleaning makes the treatment last longer.
Do you clean condos and townhomes as well as houses? Yes, all three, plus villas in the community developments. Condos come with their own wrinkles, mainly building access, elevator use and the hours a building allows vendors on site. Tell us the rules when you book and we will schedule inside them rather than arguing with a front desk on the morning.
Do you offer one-time cleaning, or is it always recurring? One-time cleans are completely normal. People book them before guests arrive, after a party, at the end of season, or just because the house got away from them for a month. There is no obligation to continue. If you do decide to go recurring afterwards, the discounts start from the next visit.
