Your business type sets the scope, not your square footage

Two suites on the same floor can need completely different work. A 2,000 square foot law office is desks, carpet, a break room and two restrooms, cleaned a few nights a week and barely thought about. A 2,000 square foot pediatric dental practice is a waiting room full of small hands, surfaces touched all day long, and a restroom in use every twenty minutes. Same footage, different job, different price.

Quotes for commercial cleaning services Boca Raton businesses ask us for start with the same question: what actually happens in the room. Medical and dental office cleaning in Boca Raton: what we do, and where we stop

Patients form an opinion of a practice in two rooms, and neither is the operatory. The waiting room and the restroom carry the whole impression. That is where a sensible scope puts its weight: reception surfaces, chair arms, door handles, light switches, restroom fixtures and floors, staff areas and the corridors between. CDC guidance on cleaning a facility keeps returning to that same short list of high-touch surfaces.

Now the honest half. We are a cleaning company, not an infection-control contractor. We do not handle sharps, regulated medical waste or instrument reprocessing, and we do not perform terminal cleaning of procedure rooms. Work covered by OSHA's bloodborne pathogens standard belongs to people trained and equipped for it, which in most practices means your own clinical staff turning over rooms between patients. We clean everything around that, to a written schedule.

Gyms, studios and salons: the rooms that never quite dry out

Boca has a boutique fitness studio in roughly every plaza, and they share a problem. Bodies produce moisture, the air conditioning fights it, and rubber flooring holds whatever settles. Mirrors show every mark under those lights, which is unfortunate for a 6am class. Mats, benches, handles, bike frames and the grab points on every machine need attention far more often than a desk does. The changing area needs it more often still.

Salons run on a different clock. The floor around a chair matters at closing, not at nine in the morning, and colour finds its way onto more surfaces than seems physically reasonable. We clean the space: floors, stations, shampoo areas, restrooms, waiting seats and glass. What stays with you is anything your licensing board requires you to disinfect yourself. When we use a disinfectant we follow the label, including the contact time, which is the part almost everybody skips.

  • Studio floors, mirrors and equipment touch points
  • Changing areas, showers and lockers
  • Salon floors, stations and shampoo areas
  • Front desks, waiting seating and entry glass
  • Restrooms and restocking on your schedule
  • Water station and towel area resets

Older Boca office parks: carpet tile, tinted glass and lovebug season

A good share of Boca's commercial stock went up in the 1980s and it has a signature look: bronze tinted glass, carpet tile in the corridors, and an entry vestibule that collects half the parking lot. Sand and grit come in on shoes and settle into the carpet tile within three feet of the door, which is why entry matting and the first stretch of floor do most of the visible work in a building like that.

Then there is the outside of the doors. Lovebugs turn up twice a year, roughly in May and again in September, and they do not distinguish between a car grille and a glass entrance. The longer they sit in the sun, the more stubborn they get. Add sprinkler overspray leaving mineral spots on the lower panes, and an entrance can look neglected while the suite behind it is spotless. Tell us at the walkthrough whether the entry is yours or the building's, because that decides who pays for it.

One-off cleans: build-outs, suite changes and a space nobody opened all summer

Tenant improvement work never really stops in this city, and the clean afterwards is its own service rather than a longer version of a nightly visit. Construction dust is fine enough to hang in the air for hours, so it lands, gets disturbed, and lands again. That is why a post-construction clean is staged rather than done in one pass, and why we book it as its own job at its own price.

The other one-off is the suite that has been shut since spring. Keys came back, the thermostat went up to save money, and the doors stayed closed through a Florida summer. What you get is a fine film on every horizontal surface, carpet that smells like the building, and a break area somebody left in a hurry. We clean it out before your new tenant walks it, or before your own team comes back. It is a satisfying job and a genuinely useful one.

Office cleaning Boca Raton quotes, and what moves the number

Every quote starts with a walkthrough, because nothing about a commercial building survives being described over the phone. We look at square footage, restroom count, the mix of hard floor and carpet, how many people come through the doors, what hours we can work, and what you want done nightly, weekly, or twice a year. Then you get one flat figure with a written scope beside it, so nobody is interpreting anything six months from now.

Ask for an office cleaning quote Boca Raton businesses can compare like for like: same scope, same frequency, same square footage. Then a Boca Raton janitorial service is easy to judge. Before the first shift we need three things from you: a named person to call, whatever access the building requires, and a short list of what must never be moved. The last one saves more trouble than the other two combined.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle medical waste, sharps or biohazard cleanup? No. That work sits under OSHA's bloodborne pathogens standard and belongs to contractors trained and equipped for it. We clean waiting rooms, reception, restrooms, staff areas, corridors and general surfaces in a practice. If a job needs something beyond that, we will tell you rather than take it on and improvise.
Can you clean a fitness studio or salon between classes and appointments? Usually the honest answer is a short daytime reset plus a full clean after close. Trying to deep clean a studio floor between a 5pm and a 6pm class helps nobody. Tell us your class or booking pattern at the walkthrough and we will build the schedule around the gaps you actually have.
How many nights a week does a small Boca Raton office really need? Most small professional suites do fine on two or three nights with restrooms and trash on every visit. Anything with public foot traffic, food, or more than about fifteen people usually needs nightly attention on restrooms at minimum. We would rather quote the frequency your traffic justifies than sell you five nights you do not need.
Do we have to sign a long contract? We would rather earn the second month than lock in the twelfth. Terms are agreed in writing with the scope so both sides know what was promised, and we are happy to talk through length before you commit. Our satisfaction guarantee applies here too: tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean at no charge.
Can you strip, wax or refinish our floors? Show us the floors when we walk the building. Hard floor care is quoted separately from a nightly scope because the work, the timing and the price are all different. If a floor needs a refinishing specialist, we will point you toward one instead of improvising with the wrong machine.
We are moving to a new suite in Boca Raton. Can you clean both ends? Yes, and it is worth booking both at once. The old suite usually needs a clean before the keys go back, and the new one is far easier to clean before your furniture lands in it. If the new space has just come out of a build-out, that is a post-construction clean rather than a standard one, and we price it that way.
