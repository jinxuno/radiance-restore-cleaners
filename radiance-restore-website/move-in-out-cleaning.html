Frequently Asked Questions

Should I book before or after the movers?

An empty home (before move-in or after move-out) allows for the most thorough clean, and is required, see the note above.

Will this help with my security deposit?

A detailed move-out clean addresses most common landlord deduction points, though we can't guarantee deposit outcomes.

Do you clean inside all the appliances, including the fridge?

Yes, this is the one service where the refrigerator interior is included automatically, not an add-on.
