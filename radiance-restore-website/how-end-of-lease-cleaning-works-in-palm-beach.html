How End of Lease Cleaning Works in Palm Beach, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›End of Lease Cleaning

Detailed cleaning to meet end-of-lease requirements and help you recover your deposit. Interiors of cabinets and appliances, floors, baseboards, and bathrooms brought back to move-in condition.

Three groups book this clean, and they all want the same result. A tenant handing back keys wants the walkthrough over quickly. A property manager wants the unit ready to show the same week it comes back. An owner selling after a tenancy wants clear photographs and a clean first impression. The job does not change between them. The home is empty, the power and water are still on, and the crew cleans every surface that a year of furniture has been sitting on. In Palm Beach that window is narrow, because lease end dates cluster around the season and the next showing is usually booked before the truck pulls away.

What's Included

  • Inside of every cabinet and drawer wiped out, along with the doors, handles and shelf edges
  • Refrigerator cleaned inside and out, oven interior wiped down, stove top and drip pans degreased
  • Baseboards hand-washed and scrubbed, doors and door casings cleaned including the hinges and jambs
  • Bathtubs, showers, shower doors, tile walls, toilets and vanities cleaned and disinfected, chrome shined
  • Ceiling fans, light fixtures, air vents, light switches and outlets dusted and wiped by hand
  • Interior windows cleaned streak-free, blinds and shutters hand-washed, every accessible floor vacuumed and mopped
  • Closet floors and shelves vacuumed and wiped, including the corners a dresser has been covering
  • Walls spot-treated for scuffs and marks, window sills and ledges cleaned, switch plates wiped

Working an Empty Unit From the Ceiling Down

The order matters more than the products do. We start dry and high: ceiling fans, light fixtures, air vents and door casings, then blinds and shutters washed by hand rather than dusted. Cabinets and drawers come next, vacuumed out before anything wet touches them, so grit is not smeared into a paste along the shelf edge. Kitchen appliances follow, with a degreaser left to sit on the oven interior and the drip pans. Bathrooms come after that, and floors go last.

What takes the time is the hand work. Baseboards get washed on hands and knees, not swiped with a mop head. Shower door tracks, faucet bases and the seal around the refrigerator door each need a cloth and a few minutes. Cheaper turnover cleans skip exactly these items: the inside of the drawers, the top of the door casings, the closet floors, the corner behind where the couch stood. Those are the first places a leasing agent checks.

Why This Matters in Palm Beach

Palm Beach runs on a seasonal calendar, and leases follow it. Plenty of tenancies here are written to end when the season does, which means handover dates are fixed months ahead and several units in the same building change hands the same week. Nobody gets to slide the date. The agent has the next showing booked, the incoming tenant has movers scheduled, and the cleaning lands in the gap between them. That gap is often a single day. Booking early is the practical answer, because the last week of a season is the week every cleaner in the area is already spoken for. The work is also far easier once the place is clear. We clean after the furniture is gone, so nothing has to be worked around: the inside of the kitchen cabinets, the refrigerator, the oven, the baseboards down the hallway, the closet floors. The home has to be vacant with the electricity and water on, since we run appliances and need hot water for the floors and bathrooms.

What to Have Ready Before the Crew Arrives

Two things make or break the appointment. Leave the electricity and water connected until after we have been through, because half of this checklist depends on hot water and working appliances. And empty the place completely, including the garage shelving, the patio, the attic hatch and the top of the hall closet. Anything left in the house has to be worked around, which is the one problem this service exists to avoid.

Ask your landlord or property manager for their move-out checklist before you book, then send it over with your square footage. If something unusual is on it, tell us in advance rather than on the day. Leave a day of margin between the clean and the inspection if the schedule allows. If anything gets missed, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean that area free of charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the home need to be completely empty before you clean? Yes. End of lease cleaning is only performed on vacant homes, with the electricity and water turned on. Furniture and boxes sit on top of the exact surfaces a landlord inspects, and without working power and hot water we cannot finish appliances, bathrooms or floors to the standard the job needs.
Will this guarantee I get my full security deposit back? No cleaner can promise that. Deposit decisions belong to your landlord and can involve damage, normal wear, or unpaid charges that have nothing to do with cleaning. What we control is the clean itself, done to move-in condition. If we miss something, tell us within 24 hours and we re-clean it free.
Is the inside of the refrigerator included in this service? Yes. On end of lease cleaning the refrigerator interior and the inside of all cabinets and drawers are included, on top of everything in our deep cleaning checklist. That is the main difference between this booking and a standard or deep clean, where the fridge interior is an add-on.
Who usually books this, the tenant or the landlord? Both do, in roughly equal numbers. Tenants book it to hand the place back in the condition the lease asks for. Landlords and property managers book it between tenancies, or when a unit comes back rough and has to be shown quickly. The scope is identical either way. Whoever books it gets the quote and arranges access for the crew.
How long does an end of lease clean take? It depends on square footage and how the home was left, so we quote flat and upfront from real measurements rather than charging by the hour. The job is staffed to finish in one visit, with the crew size matched to the size of the home. Send us the address and the layout and we will confirm timing when we hold the slot.

Lease end dates rarely move, so this is an easy job to plan around. Tell us the address, the square footage and the day the keys are due back. We quote a flat price upfront, our cleaners are insured and trained, and every job carries the 24-hour re-clean guarantee. Book end of lease cleaning in Palm Beach once your date is set.
