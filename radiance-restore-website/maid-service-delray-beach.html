What stays the same every visit, and what rotates

The value of a schedule is that the boring things are never allowed to become projects. Every visit resets the same core: kitchen, bathrooms, floors, surfaces, dust, bins. That is the spine of the arrangement, and after two or three visits it happens without discussion, in an order that suits your house rather than a generic one.

Around that spine sit the jobs that do not need doing every fortnight. Inside the oven. Inside the refrigerator. Cabinet fronts, skirting, blinds, sills, the inside of windows within reach. Those work best on rotation, one or two per visit, agreed in advance so the time is in the diary. Ask for them when you book that visit and the rest of your house does not get rushed to pay for them.

  • Every visit: kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, bins
  • On rotation: oven interior, refrigerator interior, blinds
  • On request: linens changed, cabinet fronts, skirting
  • By quote: carpet, upholstery, tile and windows
  • Never assumed: anything you would rather we left alone

Housekeeping in a downtown Delray condo

Downtown living changes the logistics more than the cleaning. Buildings around Atlantic Avenue sign vendors in at a desk, several want the service elevator booked, and a few only permit work inside set hours. Parking is its own event: the city runs garages, metered street parking and a permit system, and none of it is designed around a work vehicle arriving at eleven on a Saturday in February.

So we settle it once. Send the vendor procedure, say where a vehicle can go or which garage to use, get us added at the desk, and mention it if the association asks contractors for proof of insurance. After that the visits become unremarkable, which is the aim. Smaller units also mean shorter visits, and a lot of downtown customers run monthly rather than weekly, because there is simply less floor.

A schedule that changes twice a year

Delray keeps two calendars. A great many homes here run full from November to April and stand empty through the summer, so a recurring schedule has to bend twice a year rather than run flat. Most seasonal customers book weekly or bi-weekly while they are in residence, then pause, with two visits bracketing the gap: one to close the house up, and one timed a few days before they fly back in.

That second visit is the one people underestimate. A house shut for five months with the thermostat pushed high is stale rather than dirty, and UF/IFAS recommends keeping indoor relative humidity below 70 percent, ideally between 45 and 60, to discourage mould. Leave the air conditioning running while you are away, tell us who holds a key locally, and book the opening clean before the flight rather than after it. Your recurring discount picks up where it left off.

The part that only shows up after six months

A good housekeeping arrangement is mostly a good working relationship, and those are built out of small pieces of information. Which shower is the difficult one. Which surface marks if you look at it. The guest room that is only used at Christmas. The dog who has views about the vacuum. We keep notes on your household so that knowledge belongs to the account rather than to one person's memory.

We try to keep one cleaner on one home, since familiarity is doing most of the work here. Nobody is immune to flu or a family week away, and that is where a service parts company with a private arrangement: the day holds, cover is organised, and your notes go with the job. Whoever knocks has read them before they arrive.

Saying something when a visit is not right

Nobody enjoys this conversation, so we have tried to make it a small one. Missed something? Say so inside 24 hours and we return and re-clean that part at no charge. No form, no debate, no invoice. A text is fine. One specific sentence about one shower is far more use to us than a polite silence followed by a cancellation eight weeks later.

The wider point is that you are handing somebody a key, so it is fair to check who you are dealing with. Nobody works in a customer's home here without a personal interview, a background check, reference checks, insurance and training first. Palm Beach County also runs a Division of Consumer Affairs that investigates and mediates complaints against businesses, which is worth knowing for any service you hire, ourselves included.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a maid service in Delray Beach cost? Square footage, the state of the home and the frequency all feed into it, and the figure is flat and agreed before the first visit. A schedule takes 20 percent off weekly, 15 percent bi-weekly and 10 percent monthly. The homepage calculator will price it for you, or ring (561) 556-5899 and we will do it together.
How is this different from booking a one-off clean? A one-off clean pulls a house back from somewhere it should not have got to. Maid service stops it travelling in that direction, on a day you can plan around. Plenty of customers start with a deep clean to set the standard, then keep it there with a recurring visit.
Can I keep the schedule while I am up north for the summer? Most seasonal customers pause rather than continue. Tell us your last week in residence and roughly when you return, and we will bracket the gap with a closing visit and an opening one. Leave the air conditioning ticking over while the house is shut. The discount picks back up with the schedule.
Will the same person clean my home each time? That is the aim, and we note it on your account when it matters to you. Since illness and holidays exist, we are not going to pretend you will only ever see one face. What we can say is that anybody sent to your home arrives already knowing the routine and your household notes.
Do you work in downtown Delray buildings with a front desk? Yes. Send the building's vendor procedure, the service elevator rules, the permitted hours and any parking instructions, and add us to the front desk list. If the association wants a certificate of insurance, ask and we will provide it. Once it is set up, nobody has to think about it again.
What should I do before the housekeeper arrives? Put away anything you would not want moved or judged on our behalf, and say plainly which surfaces are fussy. That is honestly the whole list. There is no need to tidy first; that is a reflex people bring from having guests round, and this is not that.
Can I add a job to one particular visit? Yes, with a little notice so the time is allowed for. Oven interiors, refrigerator interiors, blinds and skirting are all sensible additions to a specific clean. Ask beforehand rather than on the doorstep and the extra job gets done properly instead of squeezed into the same two hours.
