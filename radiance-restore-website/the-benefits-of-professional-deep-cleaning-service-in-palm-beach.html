The Benefits of Professional Deep Cleaning Service in Palm Beach, FL

Serving Palm Beach, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Pierce

Home›Cleaning Guides›Deep Cleaning Service

A full reset for homes that need more than routine upkeep. Baseboards, door frames, vents, appliance exteriors, grout lines, and built-up buildup in kitchens and bathrooms. Recommended for first-time cleans.

Deep cleaning gets booked at recognizable moments. A first visit with a new company, when nobody knows what has been neglected. A house opening for the season after months with the shutters closed. Before a party, after a long renovation, or when the owner has finally admitted the tops of the door casings are furry. It is also what we recommend before starting a recurring schedule, because standard cleaning is designed to hold a house at a level, not to drag it up to one. A deep clean does the dragging. It takes longer, touches surfaces that never come up in ordinary upkeep, and is meant to happen once or twice a year.

What's Included

  • Baseboards hand-washed and scrubbed along the full length, not just wiped across the top edge
  • Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted and wiped down, blades done on both sides
  • Shutters and blinds hand-washed rather than dusted, plus streak-free interior windows throughout
  • Air vents, light switches, and outlet plates dusted, upholstery and furniture hand-vacuumed
  • Inside of the oven wiped out, cabinet doors, drawers, and handles cleaned front and edge
  • Doors and door casings done including hinges and jambs, walls spot-treated for marks and scuffs
  • Banisters and handrails cleaned and disinfected, exterior and top of the refrigerator wiped
  • Everything in a standard clean underneath it: floors, kitchen, baths, mirrors, trash, beds made

How a Deep Clean Reaches the Surfaces Routine Visits Miss

The difference is mostly hands and water. A blind that gets dusted looks better for a day, because dry dust moves rather than leaves. Hand-washing means each slat wiped with a damp cloth, both faces, working across the whole run, and it is slow. Baseboards get the same treatment on hands and knees, with a scrub where paint has picked up scuffs, not a quick pass along the top lip. Door casings get done including the hinge side and the jamb, which is where the grey line lives.

Sequence matters more on a deep clean than anywhere else, because so much comes down from above. Fans, fixtures, vents, and the tops of casings go first, then blinds and windows, then furniture and upholstery, then baseboards, then floors last so everything knocked loose gets picked up rather than sealed under a mop. Cheaper deep cleans skip in a predictable order: the hinge side of doors, the baseboard behind furniture, the underside of fan blades.

Why This Matters in Palm Beach

Palm Beach homes run air conditioning close to year round, and that system moves the same air through the same rooms over and over. Dust never really leaves the house, it just relocates. It builds on vent louvres and return grilles first, then fan blades, then the tops of door casings and the high ledges in rooms with ceilings too tall to reach from a step stool. Routine upkeep cannot get to any of that, and it is not supposed to. In an older estate home with heavy millwork, plaster detail, and interior shutters, the amount of horizontal surface up there is genuinely large, and it is all catching what the air handler keeps circulating. Once the layer is thick enough, every time the system cycles you get a little of it back at breathing height, which is why people describe a closed up house as smelling dusty before they see anything. A deep clean resets the loop: vents wiped, switches done, fixtures and fan blades cleaned, blinds hand-washed, baseboards scrubbed, casings taken back to the paint.

How Often a Deep Clean Is Worth Booking

Most homes book one as the first visit and then once or twice a year afterward. If the house is lived in year round with pets or children, twice is realistic, usually spring and fall. If it is a couple in a well kept house on a bi-weekly schedule, once a year is generally enough, because the recurring visits hold the ground the deep clean gained. Booking one every month is money spent on work upkeep already does.

For a seasonal home the timing writes itself. One deep clean at reopening, when blinds, fans, sills, and interior windows carry months of settled dust, and one before you close the house for the summer. If you are hiring us for the first time, book the deep clean first regardless of the calendar. It tells us what the house is really like and gives a recurring schedule a fair starting point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a deep clean include the inside of my refrigerator? No, the refrigerator interior is an add-on. A deep clean covers the exterior and the top of the fridge, the inside of the oven, the microwave inside and out, and the outside of every other appliance. Ask for the fridge interior when you book and we will quote it in with the rest.
How often does a home need a deep clean? Most homes book one as the first clean and then once or twice a year after that. A house with pets, children, or a pool usually wants two. If the property sits closed for part of the year, one at reopening and one before you head north covers it, with standard visits holding the line in between.
What does deep cleaning add over standard cleaning? Standard covers floors, surfaces, kitchen, and baths on a repeating basis. Deep adds hand-washed blinds and shutters, scrubbed baseboards, ceiling fans and light fixtures, vents, switches and outlets, door casings and jambs, walls spot-treated, interior windows done streak free, the oven interior, cabinet fronts, and hand-vacuumed upholstery.
How long does a deep clean take compared to a standard visit? Considerably longer, often two to three times the hours for the same house, because the added work is hand work on slats, baseboards, and casings rather than surfaces you pass over quickly. Exact time depends on square footage, ceiling height, and how much millwork the house has. The price is still flat and quoted before we start.
Do you clean windows during a deep clean? Interior windows only, done streak free, along with the sills and ledges. Exterior glass, screens, and anything needing a ladder outside are not part of what we do. Inside, we also hand-wash the shutters and blinds in front of those windows, which is usually what people react to when the light looks dull.

A deep clean is the visit that makes every visit after it easier. It is slower and more thorough on purpose, and it is the honest starting point for any house that has not had one in a year. If your Palm Beach home is overdue, get a flat quote from us and we will say plainly what the visit covers.
