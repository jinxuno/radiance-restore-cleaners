Booking Boynton Beach carpet cleaning around a date that will not move

Start with the calendar. When do the keys change hands, when do the movers come, and when does somebody have to sleep in the room. Everything else follows from that. We also need the practical details: who lets us in, whether the power and air conditioning are on, and whether anybody else will be working in the property that day. Two trades in one small villa is a long afternoon for everyone.

Then the rooms. Sizes, stairs, what is staying in them, and photos of anything that worries you. From that we quote a flat price before we book anything. Turnover week: one tenant out, another one in on Friday

The gap between tenants is short and it is the cheapest week you will ever get. Empty rooms clean faster, cost less and finish better, because nothing has to be lifted, shuffled and put back. If you can push the carpet work into the days when the property is bare, do it. That single decision saves more than any discount we could offer you.

Order matters too. Carpet generally goes last, after the cabinets, dusting and bathrooms are done, so that nobody walks a clean floor while carrying a bucket. Then it wants a stretch of quiet before furniture returns. We agree that sequence with you in advance. If we are doing the whole turnover, house clean and carpet together, it goes on one quote and one schedule.

What a landlord can fairly expect from a carpet

Carpet has a working life, and no amount of cleaning adds years back onto it. Flattened pile in a hallway, a seam that has given up, sun bleaching along a slider: those are wear, not dirt. We will tell you when a floor has reached the end rather than take payment for a result nobody can produce. Landlords tend to prefer that answer, even when it is the expensive one.

The deposit question is a lease question. We are cleaners, not lawyers, and we will not tell you what your agreement allows. The Florida Bar publishes a plain-language guide to the rights and duties of tenants and landlords, which is worth reading before a disagreement rather than during one. Whichever side you are on, photograph the rooms before and after. It settles most arguments in about ninety seconds.

Stairs, hallways and the first six feet inside the front door

Carpet does not wear evenly. It wears where people turn, stop and put their weight down. In the two-storey homes west of the interstate, that is the staircase, which takes more traffic per square foot than any other surface in the house and gets cleaned the least. Nearer the coast it is the strip inside the front door, where sand arrives daily and gets ground in by the next person through.

Grit is what actually shortens a carpet's life. It sits at the base of the pile and works away at the fibre every time somebody crosses the room. A doormat outside and another one inside does more good than any product on a shelf, and it is the cheapest advice in this whole trade. Regular vacuuming of the busy lanes does the rest. Periodic cleaning handles what has already worked its way down.

  • Staircases and the landing at the top
  • The mat-free stretch inside a front door
  • Bedroom doorways and the turn into a hallway
  • Under and around a desk chair
  • The floor where a sofa meets it
  • Wherever a suitcase gets parked for a week

Empty since May: seasonal rentals, storms and a carpet nobody has walked on

Plenty of Boynton properties sit closed from spring until the owners or tenants come back. What you meet on reopening is usually stale air and settled dust rather than anything sinister, and carpet holds both. Two things make the visit worthwhile: power and working air conditioning, so the rooms behave normally afterwards, and a little breathing space before anybody arrives. Booking a few days ahead of the flight beats booking the night before.

Storm season is the other variable. If water has come in through a slider, a window or a roof, that is not a cleaning job on day three, and we will say so. Wet carpet and wet padding are on a clock, and Florida Division of Emergency Management guidance is the right place to start on preparing for a season that reliably arrives. Tell us the timeline honestly and we will tell you whether you need us or a restoration company.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does carpet cleaning in Boynton Beach cost? It is priced from the rooms, the square footage, the condition and the access, quoted flat before we start. Stairs and heavily worn areas take longer and are priced accordingly. If you have several units to turn over, send them together and we will quote the batch rather than each one separately.
Can you clean between two tenants in the same week? Usually, yes, if we know early. Turnover weeks in Boynton fill up around the end of the month and around the start of the winter season. Give us the move-out date and the move-in date and we will fit the work into the gap, including evenings and weekends. We book 24 hours a day.
Should the carpet be done before or after the rest of the move-out clean? After. Cabinets, dusting, bathrooms and floors first, then carpet last, so nobody tracks over a finished floor while carrying a bucket. Then leave the rooms alone for a while before the furniture goes back. If we are doing both jobs, we sequence it for you and put it on one quote.
Can my landlord charge me for carpet cleaning? That depends entirely on your lease, and we are not the right people to interpret it. Read the clause, and read the Florida Bar guide to the rights and duties of tenants and landlords. Then photograph every room the day you hand the keys back. Evidence beats argument, and it costs nothing.
Do you work with property managers who look after several units? Yes, and it is straightforward work to schedule when the units are similar. Send addresses, unit sizes, key arrangements and the dates you are working to. We quote flat prices per unit, work whatever hours the vacancy allows, and you deal with the same family business each time rather than a call centre.
The carpet is old. Is cleaning it worth the money? Sometimes it is not, and we will say so. Cleaning lifts soil. It does not restore crushed pile, repair a failed seam or bring back colour the sun has taken. If a floor is genuinely finished, spending on a clean only delays the same decision. Send photos and we will give you an honest read before you commit.
The rental has been shut since May. What do you need from us? Power on, air conditioning working, water running, and someone able to give us access. Tell us if the property has been standing with the thermostat pushed high, and mention any leak, however small and however long ago. Those details change what we recommend, and occasionally they change who you should be calling.
