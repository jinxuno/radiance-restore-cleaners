How to use the desk

Before your first call (one time)

  1. Open the desk link Jackson sent you and bookmark it on your phone and computer. Your calls save for the whole team as soon as you log them.
  2. Type your first name in Your name in the scripts. It fills in every script for you.
  3. Install the Quo app on your computer or phone, sign in to the business line 561.556.5899, and set Quo as your default calling app. Then the green Call in Quo button rings out from the business line.
  4. Sign in to the business Gmail in the browser you use for the desk, so Open in Gmail opens the email from the business account.
  5. Keep 2 or 3 before and after photos where you can attach them to Email 3.

What you say out loud, and what you don't

Say out loudWords you speak to them, in your own voice.
For you onlyInstructions and reminders. Never read these out loud.
Send in writingEmails and texts. You send these, you don't read them on the call.

Words in bold pink inside a script fill in for you: your name, their company, their name. If you see [name], you don't know their name yet, so just skip it.

Every day

  1. Go to Call queue and pick Due today. Work from the top down.
  2. On each lead, read the yellow note and the Ask for line first. Check the best time to call that type under Call flow.
  3. Tap Start call, then tap the green Call in Quo button. Quo dials from the business line. If Quo doesn't open, tap Copy number and paste it into Quo.
  4. Tap who picked up, then follow the screens. Read only the Say out loud boxes to them, in your own voice. The For you only boxes are just for you. Tap the button that matches what they say.
  5. If they push back, tap They raised an objection, pick what they said, read the answer, then tap Back to where I was.
  6. Type anything worth remembering in Call notes: names, emails, sizes, what you promised.
  7. End with the button that matches how the call ended. That saves the call and schedules the next touch on the right day.
  8. If the touch says Send Email, or anyone asks for pricing, tap Email the guide on the lead. The link to the right guide for that type of company is already in the email. Tap Open in Gmail, check it, press Send, then tap I sent it.
  9. Need to text them instead? The same screen has a text with the guide link. Tap Copy text, paste it into Quo, and send it from 561.556.5899. Only text a number they publish for texts or gave you.

End of the day

  1. Due today should be empty. Anything left rolls to tomorrow at the top of the list.
  2. Check Talking for callbacks you set for tomorrow.
  3. Text Jackson every Booked with the date, address and contact so it goes into Jobber.

Quick answers

  • Logged the wrong button? Write the correction in Notes, then use the buttons on the card to put it right, or tap Reopen today on a closed lead.
  • Met a new business? Use Add a lead at the bottom. It starts at touch 1 today.
  • Someone says never call again? Tap Do not contact. We never reach out to them again.
  • Calling hours: weekdays 9 AM to 5 PM. Airbnb managers are also fine Sunday afternoon.

Call flow: 7 touches over 3 weeks

Every lead runs the same 7 touches. Log each touch and the desk schedules the next one. The moment someone talks to you, they leave the flow and you follow up on the day you agreed. After touch 7 with no answer, the lead rests for 60 days and comes back for a season check in.

Rules

  • Log every touch the same day, even no answers. That is what keeps the flow on schedule.
  • Never more than one call a day to the same company, and never more than 7 touches in a row.
  • Not interested means closed. Take me off your list means Do not contact, and we never reach out again.
  • Text only a number the company publishes for texting, or one a person gave you on a call.
  • Every yes becomes a booked walkthrough, a trial clean, or a date on the schedule. Write it in the notes.
  • Send the service guide for their type of company with Email 2, or as soon as anyone asks for pricing. Tap Send the guide on the lead.
