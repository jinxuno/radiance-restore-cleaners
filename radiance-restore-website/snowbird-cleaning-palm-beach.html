Snowbird Season in Palm Beach Runs on a Calendar

The first arrivals show up in early October. The numbers build through mid-November. December to March is the peak, with January and February the heaviest months of the year. Most seasonal residents head north again by late April, though a few hold out into early May. By one estimate, more than 810,000 seasonal travelers come to Florida each year, so you are not the only one calling.

That matters for booking. Everyone wants the same two weeks, the ones on either side of Thanksgiving and the stretch in April right after Easter. Seasonal resident cleaning in Palm Beach gets tight in those windows. Give us a date range as soon as your flights are ticketed and we will hold it. If the flights move, tell us and we move with them.

  • Early October: first arrivals, mostly part time residents with open schedules
  • Mid-November: the build, and the first real booking crunch
  • December to March: peak season, January and February heaviest
  • Late April: most seasonal residents fly north
  • Early May: the stragglers, and the last departure cleans of the year

The Departure Clean: Book It Before You Fly North

This is the clean you book in the last week or two before you leave. It is a deep clean with a specific purpose: take out everything a closed Florida house can feed on all summer. Crumbs behind the toaster, a film of cooking grease on the range hood, the shampoo residue in the shower corner. Organic material plus six months of heat and humidity is how a house turns on you.

We also strip the beds, wipe out the refrigerator, and get the kitchen and bathrooms down to bare surfaces. What we do not do is touch your thermostat, your water meter or your breaker panel. Those are yours, and the University of Florida has a checklist for them. If you want the full room-by-room breakdown of the leaving clean, we wrote a whole page on it.

The Arrival Clean: Land in a House That Smells Like Nothing

A house that has been shut since April does not smell bad, exactly. It smells still. Closed air, dust that settled in June and never moved, a faint something in the drains. The arrival clean is booked for the day before you land, or the morning of if your flight is late. Floors, every horizontal surface, bathrooms, and the kitchen so you can actually make coffee in it.

Dust in a closed house near the water is heavier than northern dust. There is salt in it, and it sticks. That is why an opening clean usually takes longer than people expect, and why we would rather have the house to ourselves for a full day than squeeze it into three hours. Our Treasure Coast page goes deep on the open-up sequence if you want the order of operations.

Booking a Clean Around Flight Dates

We are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, which sounds like a marketing line until your flight lands at 11pm on a Sunday in November. Tell us the arrival time and we will finish before it. Same on the way out: if the car service is coming at six in the morning, the departure clean happens the day before.

Access is the other half of it. Most part time resident cleaning in Palm Beach happens in gated communities and buildings with a front desk, and every one of them has its own rules about who gets on the list and how far ahead. Tell us how we get in when you book, and give your gate or management office our name early. Our cleaners are background-checked, reference-checked, interviewed in person, insured and trained.

  • Give us a date range as soon as the flights are ticketed
  • Tell us the arrival time, not just the date
  • Add us to the gate or front desk list a week ahead
  • Say how the crew gets in: key, code, concierge, neighbor
  • Mention now if a mid-season clean is likely later

Humidity While You Are Away Is a Homeowner Job, Not a Cleaning Job

A lot of companies quietly imply they will mind your house all summer. We do not. Radiance Restore is a cleaning company. We are not a home-watch service, not a property manager, not an HVAC contractor and not a mold remediation firm. We clean before you leave and we clean before you come back, and we will come out mid-season if you ask us to.

The moisture side is homeowner advice, and UF/IFAS Extension is the source worth reading. Their guidance for closing a seasonal home is to set the air conditioning no higher than 85 degrees so it cycles and pulls moisture out, and to keep indoor relative humidity under the 55 to 60 percent range where mold and bacteria get going. Their Palm Beach County office publishes a shorter version. Read both, then decide what you want handled and by whom.

  • Homeowner tasks, per UF/IFAS, not services we perform:
  • Air conditioning set no higher than 85 F so it cycles and dries the air
  • Indoor relative humidity kept under 55 to 60 percent
  • Refrigerator emptied and wiped, doors left slightly ajar
  • Drain stoppers in and toilets sealed, so sewer gas and pests stay out
  • Water off at the meter unless the irrigation needs it
  • Appliances unplugged before storm season

Frequently asked questions

When should I book my snowbird cleaning in Palm Beach?

As soon as your flights are ticketed. October through November and the second half of April are the tightest windows of the year, because everyone is moving at once. If you only have a rough week, give us that and we will pencil it in, then firm it up when the airline stops changing your itinerary.

Do you check on the house while I am up north?

No. We are cleaners, not a home-watch or property management service, so nobody from our team is stopping by on a schedule to look things over. What we can do is come out for a single mid-season clean if you ask, say a week before family borrows the place in July.

Can you clean the same day I fly in?

Usually, yes. We are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so an early-morning finish ahead of an afternoon landing is normal in season. Give us the flight time rather than just the date, and tell us how the crew gets in if you will still be in the air.

Do you clean for seasonal residents outside Palm Beach?

Yes. We cover most of Palm Beach County, from Jupiter and Juno Beach down through West Palm Beach, Wellington, Lake Worth, Boynton Beach and Delray Beach. We also work north into St. Lucie County, in Port St. Lucie and Fort Pierce, and south into Broward County towns like Coral Springs and Deerfield Beach.

How much does snowbird house cleaning in Florida cost?

It depends on square footage and whether the visit is a standard clean or a deep one, so we quote flat and upfront rather than by the hour. The instant calculator on our homepage will give you a number right now, and the pricing page explains what moves that number up or down.

Should I get the house cleaned before hurricane season starts?

It is a sensible way to time the departure clean. The Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30, and most seasonal residents are gone before it opens. Getting the house cleaned and closed up in April or early May means one less thing on the list when a storm shows up in August.
