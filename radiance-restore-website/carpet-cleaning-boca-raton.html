Dogs, cats and the carpet they have quietly claimed

Every pet household has one patch of floor that belongs to the animal. The spot by the sliding door where the afternoon sun lands. The corner of the bedroom. The landing with a clear view of the front path. That is where hair, dander and whatever came in from the yard get worked down into the pile. It is also why a room can look perfectly fine from the doorway and rather different from your knees.

Vacuuming is the foundation, and the CDC gives the same advice in its flea guidance: vacuum carpets and rugs often, and wash pet bedding. We are cleaners, not exterminators. If there are fleas in the house, get pest control involved first, because cleaning carpet during an active infestation is spending money in the wrong order. Mention odour when you ask for the quote rather than on the day, and you will get a straight answer about it.

The household that sneezes: what carpet cleaning can and cannot do

Carpet gets blamed for a lot of Florida sniffling. What is fair to say is that soft flooring holds whatever settles on it. The EPA lists dust mites and pet dander among the ordinary biological sources of indoor pollution, alongside the damp that a South Florida summer supplies free of charge. Carpet is one of the places all of that comes to rest between vacuums.

Our position, plainly. Periodic cleaning lifts soil that a vacuum has stopped reaching. It is not a treatment, we will not describe it as one, and anybody in this trade who tells you a clean cures an allergy is selling something. If somebody in the house has real sensitivities, say so when you book.

Area rugs and wall-to-wall carpet are not the same job

Boca floors are layered. Tile or wood underneath, then a rug that cost more than the sofa above it. Rugs are close to their own trade. Wool, silk, hand-knotted, antique, or anything with dye that might travel needs a rug specialist, and often needs to leave the house to be done properly. We will say so at the quote stage rather than work that out on your living room floor.

There is a second thing under a rug: the floor, and whatever the rug has been holding against it. If a rug or its pad gets wet, both need drying, not only the surface you can see. The EPA mould course is blunt that carpet backing and padding must be dried along with the carpet or mould is the likely result, and it puts the window at 24 to 48 hours. In August, in a house set to 78, that window is not generous.

  • Runners in hallways and on stairs
  • Machine-made rugs laid over tile or wood
  • Wool, silk and hand-knotted rugs, which we refer on
  • The pad underneath, which is part of the job

Carpet in a Boca Raton condo: bedrooms, corridors and the neighbour below

Condo living changes the conversation. In plenty of buildings the tile stops at the bedroom door on purpose, because association documents often require upper-floor units to keep most of the floor soft for sound. That means the carpet you own is carpet you cannot simply tear out the moment it looks tired. Cleaning it is not a delaying tactic. For a lot of owners here, it is the whole plan.

Access is the other half of a condo job. Buildings sign vendors in, some want the service elevator, and a few permit work only inside set hours. Send the building rules with your quote request, put us on the front desk list, and the day passes without anybody noticing. Being reachable at any hour helps when the permitted window starts at seven in the morning.

Commercial carpet cleaning in Boca Raton FL: offices and suites

Offices wear carpet differently from houses. The traffic is concentrated and predictable, and the damage is done by the same twenty pairs of shoes crossing the same short stretch between the door and the desks. Waiting rooms, conference rooms and the strip in front of the copier go first, usually while the far corners of the suite still look almost new.

Commercial carpet cleaning in Boca Raton FL is quoted once we know the area, the layout and when the space is actually empty. Most of it happens in the evening or at a weekend, because carpet wants to be left alone for a while and a Tuesday afternoon does not offer that. Tell us the square footage, whether it is broadloom or carpet tile, and what your building asks of a vendor.

Whether it is one bedroom or a whole suite, the terms are the same. A flat price quoted upfront. Cleaners who are background-checked, reference-checked, interviewed in person, insured and trained. And a guarantee with a deadline on it: say so inside 24 hours and we return and put the job right for nothing. We work across Palm Beach County and answer the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does carpet cleaning in Boca Raton FL cost? It depends on the rooms, the square footage and the condition, so we quote it directly rather than publish a number that would be wrong for half the people reading it. The calculator on our homepage prices house cleaning, not carpet. Send the rooms and a couple of photos to admin@radiancerestore.org and we will come back with a flat figure.
Will cleaning get rid of the dog smell? Sometimes completely, sometimes partly. Cleaning deals with what sits in the fibre. If liquid went through the backing and into the pad, the smell tends to come back on a humid day, and the honest answer then is that you have a bigger job than a clean. We will look and tell you which of the two you have.
Can you clean a wool or antique rug? For wool, silk, hand-knotted or antique pieces we would rather point you at a rug specialist, since those usually need cleaning off-site by somebody who does nothing else. Machine-made rugs and runners are generally straightforward. Send a photo of the back and any label, and we will tell you which category yours falls into.
How often should carpet be cleaned in a house with pets? There is no interval we can honestly quote you, because a retired greyhound and two labradors who swim daily are not the same household. Most pet owners here land on once or twice a year, with the busy rooms done more often than the guest bedroom. What you do between cleans matters more than the interval you pick.
Do you clean carpeted stairs and closets? Yes, and please mention them when you ask for a quote. Stairs take longer per square foot than any open room, because every tread is an edge. Closets need clearing before we arrive. Neither is a problem in itself. Both are awkward when they appear as a surprise on the morning of the job.
Do you do commercial carpet cleaning in Boca Raton for offices? Yes. Offices, suites, waiting rooms and studios, priced from the area and the layout, and usually done outside working hours. We also take tile, window, upholstery and janitorial work, so a whole premises can sit on one quote. Tell us the size of the space and the hours when it is empty.
